Frequently asked questions

Which heart-rate monitors work with the app?

Any Bluetooth LE heart-rate monitor that transmits the standard Heart Rate Service works — including Polar (H10, H9), Garmin (HRM-Pro, HRM-Dual), Wahoo, Coospo, and most chest straps. Chest straps are recommended over wrist optical sensors because HRV analysis needs beat-to-beat accuracy. See How It Works for details.

The app can't find my heart-rate monitor

  • Make sure Bluetooth is enabled and the app has Bluetooth permission.
  • Moisten the electrodes and wear the strap snugly — most straps only advertise when they detect a heartbeat.
  • Disconnect the strap from other apps or watches first; most straps allow only one Bluetooth connection at a time.
  • Replace the strap battery if pairing is intermittent.

Can I use the app without a heart-rate monitor?

Yes. Guided breathing sessions work without any device — you just won't see live heart-rate coherence feedback or HRV scores.

How do I delete my data?

All heart-rate and session data is stored only on your device. Use Reset History in the Breath History screen, or uninstall the app to remove everything. See our Privacy Policy.
