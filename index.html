The three things to know

01 · Physiology

Your heart rate naturally oscillates with your breath

Inhale, it speeds up. Exhale, it slows. This is respiratory sinus arrhythmia (RSA) — a direct readout of vagus-nerve activity. A bigger RSA oscillation means better autonomic flexibility. More on RSA →

Your cardiovascular system has a resonant frequency — set by the baroreflex loop between your heart and arteries. Breathe there and the RSA oscillation amplifies dramatically. Common questions

What is resonance frequency breathing?

Resonance frequency breathing means breathing at the exact pace that matches the natural oscillation frequency of your cardiovascular baroreflex loop. At that pace, respiratory sinus arrhythmia amplifies dramatically — your heart-rate variability peaks, and the HRV signal becomes a single clean oscillation (coherence). For most adults this falls between 4.5 and 6.5 breaths per minute, but the precise value is individual.

Why isn't 6 breaths per minute right for everyone?

"6 BPM" is a population average from early research, not an individual prescription. Your resonance frequency is set by the delay in your own baroreflex loop — the feedback system between blood pressure and heart rate. That delay varies with body size, age, and cardiovascular fitness. Using the wrong pace produces significantly less coherence. Individual resonance frequencies range from roughly 4.5 to 7 BPM.

What sensor do I need?

Any Bluetooth Low Energy chest-strap heart-rate monitor that transmits R-R intervals: Polar H10, Polar H9, Garmin HRM-Pro, Garmin HRM-Dual, Wahoo TICKR. Wrist-worn optical sensors are not accurate enough for real-time HRV biofeedback — they introduce enough noise to corrupt the R-R interval data the assessment depends on.

How is this different from HeartMath?

HeartMath uses a proprietary single-number coherence score and prescribes a general breathing pace. Heart Resonance measures your individual resonance frequency, shows all six Shaffer coherence criteria in real time, works with any standard Bluetooth chest strap rather than proprietary hardware, and is a one-time purchase — not a subscription.

How long until I see results?

Eight to ten weeks of 20-minute daily sessions at your resonance frequency have repeatedly produced measurable improvements in baseline HRV, baroreflex sensitivity, and vagal tone in clinical research. This is a physiological training protocol — it changes your autonomic nervous system over time, not just in the moment.
